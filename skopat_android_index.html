🥕 About SKOPAT

SKOPAT is a plant-learning website concept made for Android phones.

Current prototype includes plant search, a photo upload interface, plant fact cards, and a quiz with points.

Real photo recognition needs an AI/API connection and internet access. The included plant facts are a small sample that can be expanded.
